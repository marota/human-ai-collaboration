## How automation reshapes expert judgement

- **Bainbridge, L.** — *Ironies of Automation.* The founding text: automating the
  easy parts leaves operators with the hard parts, and less practice at them.
- **Rasmussen, J.** — *Cognitive Systems Engineering* / the **Decision Ladder**.
  Born from analysing nuclear control-room operators; shows experts take intuitive
  shortcuts rather than climbing every rung, which is why support tools have to be
  designed against real reasoning rather than idealised procedure.
- **Lundberg, J. & Johansson, B.** — *Joint Control Framework* /
  [*Modelling operator control work across traffic management domains*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10111-020-00637-w).
  Extends the ladder from one operator to human–AI teams, making the seams of
  collaboration visible.
- **Endsley, M.** — *Designing for Situation Awareness.*
- *Thinking — Fast, Slow, and Artificial: How AI is reshaping Human Reasoning and
  the Rise of Cognitive Surrender* —
  [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6097646)
- *Sycophantic AI decreases prosocial intentions and promotes dependence*
- *Faut-il encore décider ? La décision humaine à l'ère de l'intelligence artificielle*
- *Ce que l'intelligence artificielle fait de l'homme au travail. Visite sociologique d'une entreprise*
- *Deskilling, Upskilling, and Reskilling: a Case for Hybrid Intelligence*

## Regulation, assurance and validation

- **NIST** — *AI Risk Management Framework.*
- **EASA** — *Artificial Intelligence Concept Paper, Issue 2: guidance for Level 1
  and 2 machine-learning applications.* One of the few sector regulators to have
  written down what human oversight has to look like in practice.
- **EUROCONTROL** — *European Operational Concept Validation Methodology (EOCVM).*
- **CIGRE** — *The impact of the growing use of AI in the operation and control of
  power networks from an operational perspective* (Technical Brochure, 2024).
- *Perspectives on future power system control centers for energy transition.*

## On designing evaluations themselves

- **Pavão, A., Guyon, I., Viegas, E., eds.** (with contributions from A. Marot) —
  *[AI Competitions and Benchmarks: The Science Behind the Contests](https://ai-competitions-book.github.io/)*,
  ChaLearn, under review at DMLR. Chapter 5 covers turning challenge results into
  benchmarks with lasting impact.

  Competition Track — what the campaigns did and did not measure.
