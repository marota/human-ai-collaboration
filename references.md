## How automation reshapes expert judgement

- **Bainbridge, L.** (1983). *Ironies of Automation.* [Automatica, 19(6), 775–779](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0005109883900468).
  The founding text: automating the easy parts leaves operators with the hard
  parts, and less practice at them.
- **Endsley, M. R.** (2023). *Ironies of artificial intelligence.*
  [Ergonomics, 66(11), 1656–1668](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00140139.2023.2243404).
  Bainbridge's argument revisited for probabilistic, conversational systems: the
  confidence trap and the sycophancy flaw are new failure modes, not old ones.
- **Endsley, M. R.** (2011). *Designing for Situation Awareness: An Approach to
  User-Centered Design*, 2nd ed. CRC Press.
- **Shaw, S. D., & Nave, G.** (2026). *Thinking — Fast, Slow, and Artificial: How
  AI is Reshaping Human Reasoning and the Rise of Cognitive Surrender.*
  [SSRN preprint 6097646](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6097646).
  Proposes artificial cognition as a "System 3", and names *cognitive surrender*:
  adopting AI output with minimal scrutiny. Three preregistered experiments,
  N = 1,372.
- **Liu, G., Christian, B., Dumbalska, T., Bakker, M. A., Dubey, R., et al.**
  (2026). *AI Assistance Reduces Persistence and Hurts Independent Performance.*
  [arXiv:2604.04721](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04721) · [project page](https://ai-project-website.github.io/AI-assistance-reduces-persistence/).
  Randomised controlled trials, N = 1,222. Ten minutes of assistance is enough to
  degrade unassisted performance and increase giving up.
- **Cheng, M., Lee, C., Khadpe, P., Yu, S., Han, D., & Jurafsky, D.** (2026).
  *Sycophantic AI decreases prosocial intentions and promotes dependence.*
  [Science, 391, eaec8352](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aec8352).
  Eleven leading models endorse users' choices about 50% more often than other
  people do — and users prefer, and trust, the models that do it most.
- **Hazan, É., & Sibony, O.** (2026). *Faut-il encore décider ? La décision
  humaine à l'ère de l'intelligence artificielle.*
  [Flammarion](https://editions.flammarion.com/faut-il-encore-decider/9782080146687).
  Not whether human decision-making is still *possible*, but whether it is still
  *necessary* — and what commitment, authority and moral judgement add to
  efficiency.
- *Ce que l'intelligence artificielle fait de l'homme au travail. Visite
  sociologique d'une entreprise.* — référence à confirmer.
- *Deskilling, Upskilling, and Reskilling: a Case for Hybrid Intelligence.* —
  référence à confirmer.

## Making the joint decision visible

- **Rasmussen, J.** (1976). *Outlines of a Hybrid Model of the Process Plant
  Operator.* In Sheridan, T. B. & Johannsen, G. (Eds.), *Monitoring Behaviour and
  Supervisory Control*, Plenum — the origin of the **Decision Ladder**. Extended
  in **Rasmussen, J., Pejtersen, A. M., & Goodstein, L. P.** (1994).
  *[Cognitive Systems Engineering](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Cognitive+Systems+Engineering-p-9780471011989)*, Wiley.
  Born from analysing nuclear control-room operators; shows experts take
  intuitive shortcuts rather than climbing every rung, which is why support tools
  have to be designed against real reasoning rather than idealised procedure.
- **Lundberg, J., & Johansson, B. J. E.** (2021). *A framework for describing
  interaction between human operators and autonomous, automated, and manual
  control systems.*
  [Cognition, Technology & Work, 23(3), 381–401](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10111-020-00637-w).
  The **Joint Control Framework**: extends the ladder from one operator to
  human–AI teams, making the seams of collaboration visible.

## Designing the partnership

- **Suchman, L. A.** (2007). *[Human-Machine Reconfigurations: Plans and Situated
  Actions](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/humanmachine-reconfigurations/9D53E602BA9BB5209271460F92D00EFE)*,
  2nd ed. Cambridge University Press (1st ed. *Plans and Situated Actions*, 1987).
  Technical performance is nothing without interaction quality.
- **Mackay, W. E.** (2023). *Creating Human-Computer Partnerships.* In *Computer-Human
  Interaction Research and Applications*, [Springer LNCS](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-49425-3_1)
  ([talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iGqjRTyaMTc), and
  [in French at Collège de France](https://www.college-de-france.fr/fr/agenda/cours/interagir-avec-ordinateur/les-partenariats-humain-machine-comment-interagir-avec-intelligence-artificielle)).
  Co-adaptation, discoverability and appropriability as the properties that
  separate a partnership from automation.
- **Shneiderman, B.** (2022). *Human-Centered AI.* Oxford University Press
  ([overview talk](https://youtu.be/b6Qt9mLFNOQ?si=VpSKN7CmIIefuMqs)).
  Automation and human control as two independent dimensions rather than one
  slider.
- **Miller, T.** (2023). *Explainable AI is Dead, Long Live Explainable AI!
  Hypothesis-driven Decision Support using Evaluative AI.*
  [FAccT '23, ACM, 333–342](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3593013.3594001)
  ([arXiv:2302.12389](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12389)).
  Don't hand over a verdict — help the expert form a hypothesis and surface
  evidence for and against it.
- **Wäfler, T., Hamouche, S., et al.** (2025). *The Supportive AI Framework: From
  Recommending to Supporting.* Lecture Notes in Computer Science, Springer —
  AI4RealNet.
  [Paper](https://ai4realnet.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Paper-1150-Waefler-et-al.pdf).
  Supportive AI strengthens the human's judgment; substitutive AI quietly
  replaces it — same benchmark score, opposite effect over time.
- **Leyli-abadi, M., Bessa, R., et al.** (2025). *A Conceptual Framework for
  AI-based Decision Systems in Critical Infrastructures.* AI4RealNet.
- **McDermott, P., Dominguez, C., Kasdaglis, N., Ryan, M., Trahan, I., & Nelson, A.**
  (2018). *[Human-Machine Teaming Systems Engineering Guide](https://www.mitre.org/sites/default/files/2021-11/prs-17-4208-human-machine-teaming-systems-engineering-guide.pdf).*
  The MITRE Corporation.
- *Participatory design to support power grid operators in control rooms.* —
  référence à confirmer.
- *Framework for human and AI-assistant bidirectional interaction applied to
  industrial system operations.* — référence à confirmer.

## Regulation, assurance and validation

- **NIST** (2023). *[Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)*,
  NIST AI 100-1.
- **EASA** (2024). *[Artificial Intelligence Concept Paper, Issue 2: guidance for
  Level 1 and 2 machine-learning applications](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/general-publications/easa-artificial-intelligence-concept-paper-issue-2)*.
  One of the few sector regulators to have written down what human oversight has
  to look like in practice.
- **EUROCONTROL.** *European Operational Concept Validation Methodology (EOCVM).*
  — édition à confirmer.
- **CIGRE** (2024). *The impact of the growing use of AI in the operation and
  control of power networks from an operational perspective.* Technical Brochure
  — numéro à confirmer.
