The [previous post]({{ '/series/11-two-emerging-disciplines/' | relative_url }}) named two disciplines: design autonomy to be tangible and flexible, and manage it with a dial, not a default.

Here are three European projects where this is practised with real operators, today — three of the many projects on the [ecosystem map]({{ '/map/' | relative_url }}).

## AI4REALNET: one consortium, three critical networks

Horizon Europe, October 2023 to March 2027, coordinated by INESC TEC, with thirteen beneficiaries and four associated partners. Power grids, railways and air traffic management under one roof.

- **Three interaction modes designed explicitly:** AI-assisted human control, human–AI co-learning, and autonomous AI under human supervision. The autonomy dial, built into the architecture.
- **One open-source simulation bench shared across domains** — Grid2Op, Flatland, BlueSky. From virtual to tangible, at consortium scale.
- **The frameworks of this series put to work.** The [Supportive AI Framework]({{ '/series/08-explainable-ai-is-not-enough/' | relative_url }}) and the [Joint Control Framework]({{ '/series/09-forget-human-in-the-loop/' | relative_url }}) are not left on paper: they drive design and evaluation practice, with the human at the centre.

## DIALOG: a Teamwork Assistant for air traffic control

SESAR 3 JU, 2024 to 2027, coordinated by SINTEF with Deep Blue, DSNA, ONERA, Radboud University and DFS. The assistant shares tasks with the controller according to the controller's actual state.

- It infers intent from radio calls, and workload from voice and physiology, in real time.
- In March 2026, licensed controllers tested it in simulation: less mental fatigue on routine tasks, more confidence on complex decisions.

## AI-EFFECT: the test bed that comes before the grid

Horizon Europe, October 2024 to September 2027, coordinated by EPRI Europe. This is the Eurocontrol-like piece of the [Mission Control proposal]({{ '/series/10-human-ai-needs-a-mission-control/' | relative_url }}): a Testing and Experimentation Facility for AI in the energy sector, with four nodes (DTU, TU Delft, INESC TEC, Fraunhofer) and TenneT and Enel as industrial partners. AI is tested, validated and assessed in controlled, realistic environments before it reaches the grid, including congestion management on digital twins in the Dutch node.

## What the three share

The AI adapts to the human, not the other way round — the co-adaptation of [Mackay's partnerships]({{ '/series/06-foundation-techno-centric-to-partnerships/' | relative_url }}). And the test bed comes before deployment, not after.

Three projects, three answers to the question the Mission Control post left open. And only three of many: next, the whole European map.
