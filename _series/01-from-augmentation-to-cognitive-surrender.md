What happens when experts start trusting AI more than their own judgment?

The authors of the paper *Thinking — Fast, Slow, and Artificial* use a specific term for this shift: **cognitive surrender**. An expert who routinely turns to generative AI gradually stops reasoning independently — out of cognitive comfort, or excessive trust.

One observation stood out to me: the more confident the AI sounds, the more readily humans abandon their own reasoning — even in domains where they are objectively more competent than the machine.

As a researcher in decision-support systems, I've caught myself doing exactly this — trusting AI recommendations on tool optimization before grounding them in proper benchmarks.

This may look like a weak signal. But in expert decisions — medical diagnosis, investment trade-offs, industrial system operations — we can no longer afford to ignore it.
